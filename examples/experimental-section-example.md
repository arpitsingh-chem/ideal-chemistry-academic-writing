## General methods

All reactions were carried out under an inert atmosphere of dry nitrogen using
standard Schlenk techniques unless otherwise stated. Solvents were dried by
passing through activated alumina columns (MBraun SPS-800). Reagents were
obtained from commercial suppliers and used without further purification.
Column chromatography was performed on silica gel (100–200 mesh, Merck).

¹H and ¹³C{¹H} NMR spectra were recorded on Bruker Avance III 400 and 500 MHz
spectrometers; chemical shifts (δ) are reported in ppm relative to
tetramethylsilane (TMS) as internal standard, and coupling constants (J) in
Hz. Multiplicities: s = singlet, d = doublet, t = triplet, dd = doublet of
doublets, m = multiplet. High-resolution mass spectra (HRMS) were recorded on
a Bruker microTOF-Q II spectrometer using ESI. IR spectra were recorded on a
PerkinElmer Spectrum Two (ATR) spectrometer; selected bands are reported in
cm⁻¹. Melting points were measured on a Buchi M-560 in open capillaries and
are uncorrected.

**Safety statement:** All manipulations involving [hazardous reagent, e.g.,
diazomethane or peracids] were performed in a fume hood behind a blast shield;
appropriate PPE (nitrile gloves, lab coat, safety glasses) was worn at all
times. No unexpected hazards were encountered during this study.

## Synthesis of compound 2 (general procedure)

A flame-dried Schlenk tube was charged with compound **1** (1.00 mmol,
0.212 g, 1.0 equiv.), Pd(OAc)₂ (11.2 mg, 0.050 mmol, 5 mol%), XPhos
(47.7 mg, 0.10 mmol, 10 mol%), K₂CO₃ (276 mg, 2.00 mmol, 2.0 equiv.) and dry
toluene (5.0 mL) under N₂. The mixture was degassed by three freeze–pump–thaw
cycles and stirred at 80 °C for 12 h. After cooling to room temperature, the
suspension was filtered through a short pad of Celite, concentrated in vacuo,
and purified by column chromatography (hexane/EtOAc, 9:1, Rf = 0.35) to afford
**2** as a yellow solid (0.242 g, 82%).

**2:** mp 142–144 °C. ¹H NMR (400 MHz, CDCl₃): δ 8.21 (d, J = 8.8 Hz, 2H, ArH),
7.42 (dd, J = 8.8, 2.1 Hz, 2H, ArH), 6.98 (d, J = 2.1 Hz, 2H, ArH), 3.92
(s, 3H, OCH₃). ¹³C{¹H} NMR (100 MHz, CDCl₃): δ 161.2, 144.3, 131.8, 125.4,
120.7, 111.9, 55.6. IR (ATR): ν̄ = 2224 (C≡N), 1608, 1512 cm⁻¹. HRMS (ESI-TOF):
m/z calcd for C₁₄H₁₀NO₂S [M+H]⁺ 256.0427; found 256.0431. Anal. calcd for
C₁₄H₉NO₂S: C 65.87, H 3.55, N 5.49; found C 65.72, H 3.62, N 5.41.

## X-ray crystallography

Single crystals of **2** were obtained by slow diffusion of hexane into a
dichloromethane solution at 25 °C. Data were collected on a Bruker D8 Venture
diffractometer (Mo Kα, λ = 0.71073 Å) at 100(2) K. The structure was solved by
direct methods (SHELXT) and refined on F² by full-matrix least-squares
(SHELXL-2018/3). CCDC 2400000 contains the supplementary crystallographic data
for this paper; the checkCIF report is provided in the SI (p. S22).

## Computational details

Geometry optimisations and frequency calculations were performed with Gaussian
16 (Rev. C.01) at the PBE0-D3BJ/def2-TZVP level of theory, with toluene
solvation included via the IEFPCM model. Optimised Cartesian coordinates and
total energies are listed in the SI (pp. S29–S34).
