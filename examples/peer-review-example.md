# Peer-Review Example — What a Good Review Looks Like

Below: a review of a *hypothetical* manuscript, in the exact tone and structure
that ACS/RSC/Wiley editors expect (see docs/04 + the template in
`templates/review/`). Notice: specific, numbered, proportionate, no ad hominem.

---

**Manuscript ID:** AC-2026-05678
**Title:** "Synthesis and Photophysical Properties of New β-Functionalised Porphyrins"
**Journal:** [Chemistry Journal]

## Summary

The manuscript reports the synthesis of six β-functionalised porphyrins via
palladium-catalysed C–H arylation, together with their UV–vis, fluorescence,
and DFT-characterised photophysics. The key claim is a red-shifted Soret band
(λ_max = 486 nm) and enhanced photostability relative to the parent
porphyrin. The synthetic scope (six examples, 68–82% yields) is modest but
sound, and the photophysical dataset is largely complete. The work is a solid,
incremental contribution to porphyrin functionalisation; its main weakness is
that the mechanistic claim for the red shift rests on a single computational
model without experimental corroboration.

## Recommendation

**Major revision** — the photostability claim and the charge-transfer
assignment need stronger support, and the characterisation of two compounds
is incomplete. These are fixable with moderate additional work.

## Major comments

1. **Charge-transfer assignment (Fig. 4, Scheme 2).** The TD-DFT result that
   the Soret red shift arises from intramolecular CT is plausible but rests on
   one functional (PBE0). Please (a) report a functional benchmark
   (e.g., ωB97X-D and CAM-B3LYP) for the lowest two excited states, and
   (b) provide an experimental probe — a solvatochromism study in at least
   four solvents of different polarity would directly test the CT character.
2. **Photostability claim (Fig. 5).** The 92% retention after 24 h is stated
   as "outperforming the parent porphyrin by an order of magnitude", but the
   comparison shown is at 100 mW cm⁻² only. Please add (a) the intensity
   dependence or at least a second condition, and (b) degradation
   quantum-yield values, which are the standard metric in this field.
3. **Characterisation of compounds 5 and 6.** No ¹³C NMR data are reported
   for 5–6 and no HRMS for 6. Both are required for new compounds per the
   journal's guidelines; please add the data and spectra to the SI.
4. **Yield reproducibility.** Table 1 reports 82% for compound 2; the
   Experimental Section gives no replicate information. Please state whether
   yields are isolated yields from single runs or averages, with variance.

## Minor comments

1. Fig. 1: the porphyrin numbering in the structure inset conflicts with the
   text (positions 5 and 15 vs 10 and 20 in the scheme).
2. Line 214: "unprecedented" — refs 22 and 23 report related C–H
   arylations; please soften to "to our knowledge, not previously
   demonstrated at the β,β′-positions".
3. SI: pages are not numbered consecutively; the label "Figure S1" is used
   twice (pp. S3 and S9).
4. Ref 31 lacks a DOI and is incomplete (no volume/page).
5. The TOC graphic duplicates Fig. 2A; a distinct entry conveying the
   "fused chromophore → panchromatic absorption" message would be stronger.

## Confidential comments to the editor

The work is within scope and likely acceptable after the above revisions. I
suggest [Name] or [Name] as potential reviewers for the photophysics if
further input is needed on the CT assignment. No ethical concerns noted.

*Reviewing yourself? Read your manuscript as if you wrote comments 1–5 above —
that is exactly what reviewers will do. Fix them before submission.*
