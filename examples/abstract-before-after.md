# Abstract — Before / After

A realistic case in the style of the IISER Bhopal portfolio (porphyrin
chemistry, Sankar-group flavour). The "before" fails 5 journal rules; the
"after" passes all of them.

## Before (✗ — would be returned or weaken review)

> Porphyrins are very interesting molecules that have been studied for a long
> time. In this paper, we have studied the synthesis of some novel
> porphyrin-based systems. We made a new compound 1 that shows excellent
> fluorescence properties and it can be used for sensing applications. The
> compound was characterised by NMR and other techniques. We believe this work
> is very important and will open new avenues for future research in this
> exciting field.

**Problems (cross-referenced to docs/02 §3):**
1. No quantitative results (no λ, no Φ, no detection limit) — abstracts must carry numbers.
2. Hype words: "novel", "excellent", "very important".
3. "We believe" — opinion, not evidence.
4. Vague: "NMR and other techniques".
5. No context→gap→conclusion logic; no significance beyond the field.

## After (✓ — journal-ready, 198 words)

> Panchromatic light harvesting remains a central goal in the design of
> molecular photonic materials, yet synthetic access to porphyrin arrays that
> combine broad absorption with high photostability is limited by tedious
> multistep routes. Here we report a concise one-pot synthesis of a
> β,β′-fused porphyrin–benzimidazole conjugate (PBI) via oxidative
> annulation of 2-aminoporphyrins, proceeding in 68–82% yield across six
> derivatives. PBI exhibits a substantially red-shifted Soret band
> (λ_max = 486 nm, ε = 2.4 × 10⁵ M⁻¹ cm⁻¹) and a panchromatic Q-band envelope
> extending to 780 nm, with fluorescence quantum yields up to 0.31 (τ = 8.4 ns)
> in CHCl₃. Time-dependent DFT calculations attribute the red shift to
> intramolecular charge-transfer character of the fused chromophore.
> Crucially, PBI films retain 92% of their initial emission after 24 h of
> continuous white-light irradiation (100 mW cm⁻²), outperforming the parent
> porphyrin by an order of magnitude. These results establish β,β′-fused
> porphyrin–heterocycle conjugates as a practical platform for robust
> panchromatic emitters.

**Why it works:**
- Four moves: context → gap → approach/result → significance.
- Numbers in nearly every sentence (yields, λ, ε, Φ, τ, photostability %).
- No hype words; claims match the paper's data.
- 198 words → fits ACS (≤300), RSC (≤250), and even Wiley (≤200) limits.
