## Reviewer 1

**R1.1** *"The claim that compound 7 is a single diastereomer is not supported; no NOE or X-ray data are given."*

We thank the reviewer for this important point. The diastereomeric ratio was
originally assigned from the crude ¹H NMR. To confirm the assignment
unambiguously, we have now (i) recorded a NOESY spectrum of 7 (Figure S18),
which shows a clear cross-peak between H-3 and the C-2 methyl group consistent
with the cis relationship, and (ii) obtained single crystals of 7; the
structure (CCDC 2400013) confirms the cis configuration (Figure 5C). The
Experimental Section (p. 12, lines 6–14) now reports both datasets.

**R1.2** *"Why was toluene chosen over THF as the solvent in Table 2?"*

Toluene was selected to suppress catalyst decomposition observed in THF at
80 °C (control: 22% yield with THF, Table S3, entry 6). We have added this
control and a sentence explaining the solvent choice (p. 7, lines 2–5).

**R1.3** *"The quantum yield measurement lacks a reference standard."*

We thank the reviewer for the correction. We used fluorescein in 0.1 M NaOH
(Φ = 0.95) as the reference; the calculation with refractive-index correction
is now described in the SI (p. S4), and the reported value (Φ = 0.31 ± 0.02)
was remeasured from three independent samples.

## Reviewer 2

**R2.1** *"The introduction overstates novelty — refs 12–14 already describe N–N couplings of related substrates."*

We have revised the introduction to credit refs 12–14 explicitly and to frame
our contribution as the *first base-free* variant enabling alkyne-tethered
substrates (p. 3, lines 8–15), with a new comparison Table S4 summarising
prior conditions vs. ours.

**R2.2** *"Please provide the Cartesian coordinates of the transition state."*

Done — the optimised TS geometry (imaginary frequency 148i cm⁻¹, corresponding
to the N–N bond-forming mode) and all coordinates are provided in the SI
(pp. S29–S31, XYZ file attached).

**R2.3** *"English needs careful polishing in several places."*

The manuscript has been professionally language-edited; all changes are
highlighted. We hope the revised text reads more smoothly.
