**Subject: Submission of a Research Article — "Chalcogen-Bonded Organic Frameworks from 1,2,5-Chalcogenadiazoles"**

Dear Prof. [Editor-in-Chief],

Please consider our manuscript, "Chalcogen-Bonded Organic Frameworks from
1,2,5-Chalcogenadiazoles", for publication as a Research Article in
*[Journal Name]*.

Organic frameworks built from chalcogen bonds (ChBs) have attracted
considerable interest as alternatives to hydrogen-bonded frameworks, but
their construction remains largely limited to solution-phase assemblies that
collapse upon solvent removal. In this work, we demonstrate that
3,4-dicyano-1,2,5-chalcogenadiazoles self-assemble into porous crystalline
frameworks through cooperative N···Ch chalcogen bonds, retaining porosity
(SA_BET up to 1,240 m² g⁻¹) after activation — a 15-fold improvement over the
best previously reported ChB framework. Combined crystallographic and
computational analyses identify a predictive structure–porosity relationship,
and the frameworks show selective CO₂/N₂ uptake (selectivity 34 at 298 K),
suggesting applications in carbon capture.

We believe these results will be of interest to the broad readership of
*[Journal Name]* because they establish chalcogen bonding as a practical,
designable motif for permanent porosity, a question at the interface of
supramolecular chemistry and materials science that has remained open since
the earliest ChB frameworks were reported.

under consideration elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the final
