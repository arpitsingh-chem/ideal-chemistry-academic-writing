## 2. Publisher-family requirements (the "spec")

### 2.1 American Chemical Society (ACS) — ~23 journals in portfolio

**Manuscript types & limits**
| Type | Words (incl. abstract, excl. refs) | Notes |
|------|------------------------------------|-------|
| Article | no hard limit; ~7,000 practical | Sections: Introduction, Results & Discussion, Experimental, Conclusion |
| Communication/Letter | **2,200 (JACS), 3,000 (many titles)** | ≤5 figures, ≤50 refs; no section headings |
| Review | ≤12,000 | Abstract ≤300 words |
| Perspective | ≤5,000 | ≤50 refs |

**Hard requirements (all ACS titles)**
- **Abstract + Table-of-Contents (TOC) graphic in the manuscript file** — not just in the submission system. TOC graphic: **3.25 in × 1.75 in (~8.25 × 4.45 cm)**.
- Abstracts: Articles ≤ **300 words**; Letters/Perspectives ≤ **150 words**; JACS ≤250.
- **No hype words**: "new", "novel", "first", "superb", "excellent", "exceptional", "outstanding" are restricted in titles/abstracts unless rigorously supported.
- **References numbered** (superscript or bracketed per journal); article titles required in most ACS journals now; **no excessive self-citation**.
- **Experimental Section** with full reproducibility detail + **affirmative safety statement** for any experimental work.
- Supporting Information as a **separate file**, pages numbered consecutively, labelled Figure S1/Scheme S1/Table S1; a *descriptive* SI paragraph in the manuscript ("¹H NMR spectra for all compounds (PDF)"), not "Figures S1–S3".
- **Characterisation requirements** (organometallic/inorganic): identity, purity, yield; synthetic procedures with quantities; crystallographic data → deposit with CCDC; note diamagnetic corrections (Pascal's constants) for magnetic data.
- **ORCID** required for corresponding author; **CRediT** contribution statements; equal-author statements allowed in simple form only; funder reporting; conflict-of-interest disclosure.
- Related works "in press/submitted" must be uploaded as *Supporting Information for Review Only*.
- Graphics: 300 dpi min (600 dpi line art); all graphics placed near first mention (not grouped at end) in most titles.

### 2.2 Royal Society of Chemistry (RSC) — ~25 journals in portfolio

**Manuscript types & limits**
| Type | Words | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Communication | 1,500–2,500 (NJC); ~3,000 (ChemComm) | 1 table + up to 4 figures typical |
| Full Paper | <8,000 | Experimental procedures ≤2 journal pages |
| Review | >8,000; mini-reviews ~5,000 | Proposal form for some titles |
| Edge Article (Chem. Sci.) | flexible, high novelty | "new concepts" statement required |

**Hard requirements (all RSC titles)**
- **Abstract 50–250 words** — concise, searchable, no references.
- **TOC entry**: graphic (max **8 cm × 4 cm**, TIFF/PDF, >600 dpi) + **1–2 sentence text (~20 words)**.
- **Experimental detail**: amounts in **g and mmol**, yields with how they were calculated; standard techniques stated once at the start; unusual hazards clearly identified; compounds ≥95% pure with proof for bioactivity claims.
- **Data availability**: any data required to verify the research must be available on submission (repository deposit encouraged; "data available on request" is *not* acceptable).
- **Crystallography**: CCDC numbers + **checkCIF** files; JCAMP-DX (.jdx) format encouraged for spectral data.
- **Reference style**: numbered (journal-specific); article titles required in several RSC journals (Org. Chem. Frontiers, Inorg. Chem. Frontiers, Mater. Chem. Frontiers, Food & Function).
- CRediT/author contribution statements; ORCID for corresponding author; conflict-of-interest and funding statements; ethics statements for human/animal work; MIAME compliance for microarray data.
- Errors bars + uncertainty analysis expected on quantitative data; correct significant figures.

### 2.3 Wiley-VCH / Chemistry Europe — ~20 journals in portfolio

**Manuscript types & limits**
| Type | Words | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Communication (Angew. Chem.) | **4 printed pages** (~2,500–3,000 words body) | No abstract — TOC text (450 chars) + TOC graphic (5 × 5 cm) instead |
| Research Article (Angew.) | ~5,000 words | Abstract ≤200 words; 10 printed pages |
| Research Article (Chem. Eur. J.) | 2,500–5,600 | 3–8 display items; abstract ≤200 words, no graphics/refs in abstract |
| Review / Minireview | 5,600–14,000 / 10–12 pp | 5–7 keywords; Summary & Outlook section |
| Highlight | 8,500 chars | 1–2 figures |

**Hard requirements (all Wiley chemistry titles)**
- **Abstract ≤200 words**, must be standalone: no graphics, no compound numbers, no references inside the abstract.
- **TOC graphic** (5.5 × 5.0 cm or 11.5 × 2.5 cm for Eur. JOC; 5 × 5 cm for Angew.) + text ≤450 characters.
- **CRediT author-contribution statements required** (Wiley-wide policy).
- Full characterisation for every new compound: ¹H/¹³C NMR, HRMS, IR, melting point, purity; spectra in SI.
- Experimental section must name hazardous chemicals and techniques explicitly; IUPAC names preferred, with compound numbers for convenience.
- Data availability statement + conflict-of-interest statement + funding/grant numbers mandatory.
- 5–7 keywords; ORCID required for corresponding author.
- Cover letter mandatory, stating relevance/importance, suggested reviewers, opposed reviewers.

### 2.4 Others (Elsevier, Springer, IUCr, AIP, APS)
- **Elsevier (J. Mol. Struct.)**: standard IMRaD; structured abstract optional; graphical abstract (single image, 531 × 1328 px min for most titles); highlights (3–5 bullets, ≤85 chars each); CRediT mandatory; data availability statement.
- **Springer (J. Chem. Sci.)**: abstract 150–250 words; keywords 4–6; IMRaD; no page limits for articles.
- **IUCr (Acta Cryst A)**: crystallographic papers require CIF + structure-factor data; checkCIF validation mandatory.
- **AIP (J. Chem. Phys.) / APS (Phys. Rev. E)**: physics-style papers — long "Methods", theory sections; figures in EPS/PDF; abstract ~200 words; publication charges for colour.

## 3. The common threads — what ALL 252 journals require

Synthesised from the guidelines above, an *ideal* chemistry manuscript must always have:

1. **Title** — informative, ≤18 words / ~135 characters; no hype words ("novel", "first") unless proven.
2. **Author list + affiliations** — complete, current addresses, e-mail for every author, corresponding author marked, ORCID for corresponding (and ideally all).
3. **Abstract** — 150–300 words depending on type; purpose → key results (with numbers) → conclusion; no references, no graphics; searchable keywords.
4. **TOC/graphical abstract** — required at ACS (3.25×1.75 in), Wiley (5×5 cm + 450-char text), RSC (8×4 cm + 20-word text), Elsevier (one image).
5. **Keywords** — 4–7, lowercase, from the journal's keyword list when available.
6. **Introduction** — context, gap, hypothesis, significance; non-specialist friendly.
7. **Results & Discussion** — logical order, figures at first mention, error bars, uncertainty, correct significant figures; no data duplication across figures and tables.
8. **Experimental Section** — reproducible recipes (g + mmol), characterisation of *every* new compound, hazards, instrument details; in SI for Letters.
9. **Conclusion / Summary & Outlook** — what was achieved, why it matters, what's next.
10. **References** — journal style, complete (titles, DOIs), balanced (not self-heavy), DOI links for online-only sources.
11. **Back matter** — CRediT, acknowledgements (funding + grant numbers), conflict-of-interest, data-availability, ethics (if applicable), AI-use disclosure (per journal policy).
12. **Supporting Information** — separate file, numbered pages, labelled items, descriptive SI paragraph, spectra for all compounds, checkCIF files, JCAMP-DX, coordinates.

## 4. What editors desk-reject (evidence from the portfolio journals)

- Hype in title/abstract ("first report of…" without literature support).
- Missing TOC graphic or abstract (automatic return at ACS).
- Incomplete characterisation of new compounds (most common reviewer complaint across ACS/RSC/Wiley).
- Experimental section too thin to reproduce ("the compound was prepared as before").
- No safety statement for hazardous work.
- References without titles/DOIs; excessive self-citation.
- No data-availability statement (RSC/Wiley).
- Figures below 300 dpi or placed away from first mention.
- Word count exceeding the journal's stated limit for the article type.
- "Data available on request" — not acceptable at RSC.
