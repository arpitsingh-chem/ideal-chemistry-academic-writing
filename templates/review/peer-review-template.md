## Major comments

1. **[Comment]** The claim that ... rests on ... (Fig. X). However, ...
   **Why it matters:** ... **Requested action:** please provide ... / clarify ...
2. **Control experiments:** The proposed mechanism ... but no control
   excluding ... is shown. Please add ...
3. **Characterisation:** Compound 7 is claimed as new but no HRMS data are
   reported; ¹³C NMR for 8–11 missing. Per journal guidelines all new
   compounds require full characterisation.
4. **Reproducibility:** The Experimental Section omits ... (e.g., catalyst
   loading, temperature ramp); a skilled researcher cannot repeat the
   synthesis as written.
5. **Computational:** The DFT results use ... without justification of the
   functional; no coordinates in SI.

## Minor comments

1. Fig. 3: axis labels missing units; resolution appears below 300 dpi.
2. Scheme 2: yields missing for entries 4–6.
3. References 12–15 are not cited in text order.
4. SI page numbers restart; please number consecutively.
5. Line 214: "unprecedented" is not supported by the cited prior work.
6. Typography: use correct superscripts in molecular formulae throughout
   (e.g., "CO2" → CO₂).
